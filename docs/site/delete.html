Deleting your data

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Zhuzha has no account and no server. The developer holds no copy of anything you write, read or ask, so there is nothing to request the deletion of. Every piece of your data is on your own device, and you can remove all of it yourself, at any time, without asking anyone.

Delete one book

Open the Library and tap the bin beside the book. That removes the book's chapters, pictures, notes, questions, bookmarks and handwriting from the device.

Delete everything

Uninstall the app. That removes every book, every note and the stored Gemini API key, which lived encrypted on the device and was never sent to the developer.

Backups you made yourself

If you backed your books up to a folder you chose, those files are yours and sit outside the app. Uninstalling does not touch them — delete them with any file manager. The same is true of any EPUB you exported.

Your Gemini API key

Chapters are written using a Google Gemini API key that belongs to you. Prompts sent with that key are handled by Google under Google's own terms, not the developer's: the developer never sees the key, the prompts or the replies. To delete or revoke the key itself, use Google AI Studio, and see Google's own policies for what Google retains.

If you still want to ask

If you believe the developer holds data about you, write to info@zhuzha.app and it will be dealt with. The honest expected answer is that there is nothing held to delete.
